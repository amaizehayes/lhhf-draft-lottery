## League Rules Summary

- **Format**: 12-team, Head-to-Head Each Category
- **Scoring Categories (14 total)**:
  - Hitting (7): HR, RBI, R, SB, AVG, TB, BB+HBP
  - Pitching (7): SV, HLD, ERA, WHIP, K/9, QA3, BAA
- **Roster**: 24 players max (16 active, unlimited reserve, 3 IR, 5 minors)
  - Active: C, 1B, 2B, 3B, SS, OF x3, DH, UT, P x6
- **Keepers**: 5 regular + 5 minor league
- **Keeper Cost Rules**:
  - Keepers cost the round they were originally drafted in (first time keeping)
  - Each subsequent year kept, cost escalates 3 rounds earlier (R10 → R7 → R4 → R1)
  - Cost caps at R1 (stays there indefinitely)
  - Undrafted players (waiver/FA pickups) carry a R13 keeper value
  - **Max 2 keepers in Rounds 2-4**
- **Minor League Keepers**: Free while in minors. R16 cost when promoted to big league roster
- **Minor League Eligibility**: Career AB <= 130 (hitting) or Career IP <= 50 (pitching)
- **Draft**: Offline, Snake/Serpentine, 24 rounds
- **Draft Pick Trading**: Allowed (2 future years, 24 rounds)
- **Trade Deadline**: August 21, 2026
- **Waivers**: 5 claims/week, 1-day waiver wire, priority rotation
- **Minimum IP per scoring period**: 45 IP (miss = loss for rate pitching cats)
- **Playoffs**: Start Week 24 (Sep 7), 3 rounds

### Round-by-Round Targets

**R1 (Pick 7) — Best Player Available**

With ~50-60 keepers off the board, pick 7 is effectively like pick 40-50 in redraft. But many elite R1-cost players return to the pool because teams can only keep 1 at R1.

- TIER 1 (if they fall): Kyle Tucker, Jose Ramirez, Vladimir Guerrero Jr.
- TIER 2 (realistic): **Cal Raleigh** (solves C, 30+ HR), Kyle Schwarber, Ketel Marte, Yordan Alvarez
- TIER 3 (pitching): Hunter Brown, Cristopher Sanchez
- **Recommendation**: Raleigh or best hitter. Catcher is thin — he may be the only elite C option.
